Ducati Panigale V4 S

What is this Diva ?

The Ducati Panigale V4 S represents the pinnacle of Italian motorcycle engineering, translating MotoGP racing dynamics into a production superbike. Powered by the Desmosedici Stradale engine, it integrates cutting-edge electronic rider aids and adaptive chassis control to deliver unmatched high-speed aerodynamic stability and track performance.

Technical and Mechanical Specifications

  1. Engine: 1103 cc Desmosedici Stradale 90-degree V4, liquid-cooled
  2. Power Output: 215.5 hp at 13,000 rpm
  3. Torque: 123.6 Nm at 9,500 rpm
  4. Suspension: Ohlins Smart EC 2.0 electronic suspension system
  5. Braking: Brembo Stylema monobloc calipers with dual 330 mm discs
  6. Transmission: 6-speed with Ducati Quick Shift up and down EVO 2

Aesthetic and Visual Features

  • Double-profile aerodynamic carbon-composite winglets
  • Signature twin LED headlights with integrated Daytime Running Lights
  • Sculpted aluminum fuel tank optimized for aggressive track ergonomics
  • Exposed single-sided aluminum swingarm highlighting the rear wheel
  • Aerodynamic extractor vents integrated into the fairings for heat dissipation
